## Overview

my-app authenticates users with a username and password. Login enforces three
policies, all defined in `src/auth.py`.

## Login policy

- **Account lockout** — after **5** consecutive failed login attempts, the
  account is locked and further attempts return `account_locked`.
- **Minimum password length** — passwords must be at least **8** characters;
  shorter passwords are rejected with `password_too_short`.
- **Session timeout** — a successful login starts a session that expires after
  **30** minutes of inactivity.

## Responses

| Outcome | `reason` |
|---|---|
| Locked account | `account_locked` |
| Password too short | `password_too_short` |
| Wrong credentials | `invalid_credentials` |
| Success | — (returns `session_timeout_minutes`) |
